The Treatment Menu

Every treatment starts with a quick chat about the shape you want — then it's handled there and then.

Brow Threading

Precise, clean shaping using thread for the crispest line — the fastest way to sharpen your brow.

Brow Tinting

Adds depth and definition between shapes — low-maintenance colour that holds for weeks.

Brow Waxing

Fast, precise shaping for anyone who prefers wax over threading.

Brow Lamination

Fuller, brushed-up brows that hold their shape for weeks — great for sparse or unruly brows.

Lash Lift & Tint

Lifted, darker lashes with no extensions and no daily mascara.
